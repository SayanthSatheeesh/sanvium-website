01 · Why invitations go wrong

A single "yes" feeds no one.

A beautiful card is the easy part. The hard part is everything after you press send.

300 yeses. Zero headcounts.

"Haan haan, aa jayenge" isn't a number your caterer can cook for, and a family of two quietly becomes five.

The haldi list isn't the sangeet list.

One link for everyone means awkward questions at the door, and extra plates for functions people were never invited to.

Nani won't tap a mystery link.

Police have warned about fake wedding invite files on WhatsApp. Elders are right to be careful, so your invite has to feel safe.

The hall changed. Did everyone hear?

A new time or venue is only fixed when every guest invited to that function knows, not when you post it in one group.

03 · How the guest desk works

You choose the people. We handle the follow-up.

  1. 01

    Send us the list

    An Excel sheet, your WhatsApp contacts, or a photo of the family diary. We type it up and check it with you.

  2. 02

    We design and tag

    Your invitation, designed for your family, with every guest tagged to the functions they're invited to.

  3. 03

    We send, chase and update

    Personal links go out on WhatsApp. We send polite reminders, and if anything changes, everyone affected hears about it.

  4. 04

    Your caterer gets a number

    A headcount per function, ready before the caterer's cut-off. You get your evenings back.

04 · What's handled

Everything after "send".

  • RSVP per function

    Guests choose how many people are coming to each function, not just yes or no.

  • Personal guest links

    Each family sees their own name and only the functions they're invited to.

  • Reminders that stop on reply

    Families who haven't answered get a gentle nudge. Families who have answered hear nothing more.

  • Change alerts

    A new time or hall reaches exactly the guests invited to that function, and the link updates itself.

  • Made for elders

    A simple view with large text and no animation, plus a printable PDF for the cards you still hand over in person.

  • Directions to the right door

    Not just a map pin: which gate, which hall, and where to park.

  • Arrivals and pickups

    Collect flight and train details from out-of-town guests and share a pickup sheet with your team. (Concierge)

  • A thank-you afterwards

    One message to every guest with your thanks and a link to the photos.

05 · Safe to open

No apps. No files. No strangers.

  • Opens in the browser. Your invitation is a web page with your names in the link. Nobody downloads or installs anything.
  • From a sender guests recognise. Messages come from a WhatsApp Business sender named for your wedding, never from a random number.
  • Your guest list stays yours. Phone numbers are used only for your wedding, and we delete them after it.

08 · Questions

Before you ask.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. The invitation opens in any phone browser from the WhatsApp message. Guests don't sign up or log in.

Can elders still get a printed card?

Yes. Every plan includes a printable PDF of your invitation, and the simple view is made for guests who find small text or animations hard.

What if a time or venue changes?

We update the invitation and, on Guest Desk and Concierge, send an alert to every guest invited to that function. Nobody has to reprint anything or forward a new link.

Whose WhatsApp number sends the messages?

A WhatsApp Business sender named for your wedding, so guests see who it's from. Replies come to us, and we pass anything personal on to you.

How early should we start?

About eight weeks before your first function. That leaves time to design the invitation, send it, chase replies, and give your caterer a count before their cut-off.

What happens to our guest list afterwards?

We send you a final export, then delete guests' phone numbers. Your invitation page can stay online as a keepsake if you'd like.
