PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information Sanvium Technologies ("we", "us") collects when you visit sanviumtechnologies.com and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email, company, project type, budget range and message, which you choose to send us.
  • Newsletter: your email address, if you subscribe.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) kept by our hosting provider.

How we use it

We use this information only to reply to your enquiry, send the newsletter you asked for, and keep the website secure and working. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

  • Formspree processes contact and newsletter form submissions.
  • Vercel hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts, Fontshare, jsDelivr and unpkg deliver fonts and code libraries, so your browser connects to them when loading pages.

Retention

We keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to respond and manage any resulting project. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing hello@sanviumtechnologies.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
